04HOW IT WORKS

How It Works

Three subsystems close the loop: the world comes in as electricity, electricity becomes weights, and weights become action.

SUBSYSTEM A

Sensory Encoding

OPTIC LOBE ENCODING

The compound eye's optical flow is decomposed layer by layer: the medulla extracts edges and orientation, while each T4/T5 unit swears loyalty to a single direction of motion. A fly doesn't see a picture — it sees a vector field in flux.

SUBSYSTEM B

Synaptic Plasticity

MUSHROOM BODY PLASTICITY

About 4,000 Kenyon cells weave odor cues into sparse codes; PPL1/PAM dopamine neurons act as the teachers — prediction error decides which weights rise and which fall. Sugar rewards, shock punishes, and the two signals carve opposite traces.

SUBSYSTEM C

Motor Decoding

MOTOR DECODING

The central complex issues heading and gait commands; the giant fiber pathway fires at a touch, compressing the escape jump into milliseconds; and the ventral nerve cord translates it all into every step of six legs and every beat of two wings.

06STATED PLAINLY

What It Is Not, Plainly

Stating the limits clearly is part of the experiment.

  • No consciousness. The spiking of 139,255 digital neurons is just math running. This fly has no subjective experience, no feelings, no pain.
  • Not a real fly. The structure is synthesized in code from public-connectomics priors — it is not based on scans of any real fruit fly.
  • The numbers are a demo. Firing rates, membrane potentials and transmitter levels are generated live by this page — they are not experimental records and support no scientific claim.
  • It is fully local. No server, no uploads. All computation happens on the device that opened this page — close the tab and it dies.
  • "Learning" is curve-fitting. The weights change and the behavior changes, but this remains far from anything you would strictly call intelligence.
